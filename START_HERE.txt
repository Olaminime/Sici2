The indicator automatically:

1. Scans the market for high-probability trade setups
2. Combines 5 different confirmation filters
3. Displays position boxes when opportunities appear
4. Shows exact entry price, stop loss, and take profit
5. Updates in real-time as market moves
6. Sends alerts when setups are detected
7. Manages risk with ATR-based dynamic stops


LONG SIGNAL appears when:
  ✓ Trend is bullish (EMAs aligned)
  ✓ RSI recovering from oversold
  ✓ Price near support level
  ✓ High volume confirmation
  ✓ All conditions met

SHORT SIGNAL appears when:
  ✓ Trend is bearish (EMAs aligned)
  ✓ RSI declining from overbought
  ✓ Price near resistance level
  ✓ High volume confirmation
  ✓ All conditions met
